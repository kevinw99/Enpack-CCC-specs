## Known Limitations and Gaps

### Data Quality Issues Documented

1. **MTBF for Foil Coating Equipment**: Limited specific data available
   - Industry standards exist for general manufacturing (200-500 hours new, 1,000-2,000 hours mature)
   - Specific foil coating equipment MTBF data not publicly detailed
   - Equipment manufacturers guard proprietary reliability data

2. **Competitor Specific Good Rates**: Detailed quality metrics remain proprietary
   - Only general statements available ("industry-leading quality", ">95%")
   - Specific defect rate data by competitor not in public domain
   - Estimated ranges derived from industry benchmarks

3. **Detailed Cost Structures**: Exact cost percentages proprietary
   - General ranges documented (60-75% material, 15-25% labor, etc.)
   - Geographic and company-specific variations documented but with uncertainty
   - Cost per unit highly dependent on commodity prices and facility location

4. **Geographic Cost Variations**: Limited public data
   - General patterns identified (Japan premium, China lower-cost, Korea balanced)
   - Specific cost comparisons require company-level data
   - Labor cost data from public sources (BLS, government surveys) limited for manufacturing sector

5. **Equipment Specifications**: Variable detail level
   - Some manufacturers (Tmax, Delta ModTech) provide detailed specs
   - Other suppliers' data limited or proprietary
   - Real-world performance vs. rated specifications gap documented but not quantified
