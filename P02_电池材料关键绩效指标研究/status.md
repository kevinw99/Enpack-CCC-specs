## Key Research Findings Summary

### OEE and Equipment Performance
- **Industry Standard OEE**: 85%+ for competitive operations
- **New Lines OEE**: 45-65% during ramp-up (0-12 months)
- **Mature Lines OEE**: 75-85%+ after stabilization (3+ years)
- **OEE Components** (world class): 90% Availability × 95% Performance × 99.9% Quality

### Quality Metrics
- **Good Rate - New Lines**: 70-85% (broad learning curve)
- **Good Rate - Mature Lines**: 92-97% (industry best practice)
- **Defect Rate - Mature Lines**: 2-8% (target <2%)
- **Common Defects**: Thickness variation (2-15%), delamination (3-20%), uneven coating (4-25%)

### Capacity and Utilization
- **New Equipment Speed**: 0.5-1.0 M/min optimal coating speed
- **Maximum Equipment Speed**: Up to 7.0 M/min (mechanical transport)
- **Capacity Utilization Target**: 85%+
- **New Line Utilization**: 60-75% (ramp phase)
- **Mature Line Utilization**: 85-95% (optimal range)

### Cost Structure
- **Material Costs**: 60-75% of production cost (largest component)
- **Labor Costs**: 15-25% of production cost (geography dependent)
- **Manufacturing Overhead**: 5-15% of production cost
- **Energy Costs**: 3-8% of production cost
- **Scrap/Rework**: 5-15% (worse in new lines, improves with maturity)

### Competitor Landscape
- **Japanese Leaders**: Mitsui Mining (>90% ultrathin market), JX Nippon (10.8% global share), Furukawa, Sumitomo
- **Korean Competitors**: LS Mtron/KCFT (30,000 tons), ILJIN (45,000 tons), SKC (KCFT acquirer)
- **Chinese Growth**: Jinmei, Anhui, Shanghai, Xiamen (rapid expansion phase)
- **Combined Top 8 Capacity**: 225,000-255,000 metric tons annually

### Energy Intensity
- **Full Battery Manufacturing**: 30-35 kWh per kWh capacity
- **Foil Coating Component**: 8-12 kWh per metric ton (estimated)
- **Aluminum Production**: 13-15 kWh per kg (best practice)
- **Carbon Footprint**: 150-200 kg CO2 per kWh battery capacity

### Equipment Reliability
- **New Equipment MTBF**: 200-500 hours average
- **Mature Equipment MTBF**: 1,000-2,000+ hours (world class)
- **MTTR (Time to Repair)**: 4-8 hours (new), 2-4 hours (mature)
- **Maintenance Cost**: $50-150 per operating hour

## Known Limitations

### Data Gaps
1. **Proprietary Information**: Detailed good rates, exact MTBF, specific costs remain confidential
2. **Equipment Specifications**: Limited public MTBF data for foil coating equipment specifically
3. **Geographic Cost Data**: Labor and energy costs vary; general ranges documented
4. **Competitor Details**: Public data limited; market share estimates from multiple sources
5. **New Technology Metrics**: Composite foil benchmarks still emerging; less historical data

### Research Constraints
- Academic research sometimes lags industry practice (2-3 year lag typical)
- Equipment manufacturer data often proprietary or protected
- Company filings may lack specific manufacturing metrics
- Analyst reports sometimes behind paywall (used public summaries only)
- Competitive intelligence limited to public disclosures

### Recommended Verification
- Direct engagement with equipment manufacturers for MTBF specifications
- Primary data collection from Enpack CCC operations (internal benchmark)
- Industry expert interviews for validation of estimates
- Facility tours to verify practical operating conditions
- Equipment vendor technical discussions for specifications
