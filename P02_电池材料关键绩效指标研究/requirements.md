# Requirements: Battery Material Manufacturing KPI Research

## Objective
Compile comprehensive, well-sourced industry benchmarks and competitor performance metrics for composite foil and current collector manufacturing in lithium-ion battery production.

## Specific Requirements

### 1. Capacity and Utilization Benchmarks
- Document industry standard capacity by equipment generation (new vs. mature)
- Identify typical utilization rates for new production lines (0-12 months)
- Identify typical utilization rates for mature production lines (3+ years)
- Document capacity constraints by process step
- Provide context on equipment speed limitations and practical constraints

### 2. Quality Performance Metrics
- Establish good rate targets by production maturity stage
- Define expectations for new lines (first 1-2 years) with learning curve
- Document common defect types and typical frequencies
- Create benchmarks by defect type (delamination, thickness variation, etc.)
- Include detection methods and quality control approaches

### 3. Equipment Performance
- Compile MTBF data for new lines (target 200-500 hours range)
- Compile MTBF data for mature lines (target 1,000-2,000 hours range)
- Document OEE target framework (80%+ standard)
- Break down OEE components: Availability, Performance, Quality targets
- Provide maintenance cost per operating hour estimates

### 4. Cost Structure Analysis
- Define material cost as % of total (target 60-70% range)
- Define labor cost as % of total (target 20-25% range)
- Define manufacturing overhead as % of total (target 5-10% range)
- Document energy intensity: kWh per unit area or per ton
- Provide cost reduction curves and scale economy benefits

### 5. Competitor Performance (Public Data Only)
- Identify major competitors (FURUKAWA, Sumitomo, Olin, JX Nippon, Mitsui Mining)
- Document public capacity information from annual reports
- Compile market share data where publicly available
- Analyze cost position based on publicly available information
- Focus exclusively on verified public filings (SEC, annual reports)

### 6. Information Quality Standards
- Provide confidence level (target 85%+) for each metric
- Cite all sources with full references and URLs
- Distinguish between verified data and industry estimates
- Acknowledge limitations and gaps in available information
- Clearly separate proprietary assumptions from public fact

## Deliverables

### Primary Document
- Comprehensive research summary with industry benchmarks in table format
- Competitor comparison table (public data only)
- Cost structure analysis with typical ranges and variance factors
- Quality benchmarks by product maturity stage
- Equipment performance baselines with reliability data
- Complete source documentation and citations

### Supporting Materials
- Competitor profile summaries (maximum 3 pages each)
- OEE analysis framework with calculation examples
- Cost structure sensitivity analysis
- Defect type taxonomy and quality control approaches
- Equipment MTBF reference guide

## Data Quality Criteria

### Sources to Prioritize
1. SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q forms for US companies)
2. Company annual reports and investor presentations
3. Peer-reviewed academic research
4. Industry analyst reports from established firms
5. Equipment manufacturer specifications
6. Government data (DOE, NIST) on manufacturing metrics

### Sources to Use Cautiously
- Market research reports (often behind paywall; use public summaries)
- News articles (verify with primary sources)
- Company press releases (verify independently)
- Third-party estimates (clearly label as such)

### Sources to Exclude
- Proprietary internal data
- Unverified rumors or speculation
- Single-source estimates without corroboration
- Outdated information (>3 years for rapidly changing metrics)

## Scope Limitations

### In Scope
- Composite foil manufacturing
- Current collector production
- Aluminum and copper foil for batteries
- Lithium-ion battery material processing
- Equipment reliability metrics
- Manufacturing cost structures
- Public competitor data

### Out of Scope
- Proprietary manufacturing processes
- Confidential cost structures
- Internal competitor intelligence not from public sources
- Detailed recipe/formula information
- Future product roadmaps not publicly disclosed
- Unverified technical specifications

## Confidence Target
**Minimum 85% overall confidence** in final research summary, with clearly marked confidence levels for individual metrics.

## Timeline
- Research completion: Comprehensive data collection and analysis
- Final document: Complete research summary with all citations verified
