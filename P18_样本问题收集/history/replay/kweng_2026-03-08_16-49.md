## P20: MCP 问答质量评估 (MCP Q&A Quality Evaluation)

**Core idea:** Blind A/B comparison — send the same business questions through two paths (vanilla LLM vs MCP-augmented LLM), then score answer pairs on 6 dimensions: accuracy, completeness, specificity, actionability, hallucination, and source grounding.
