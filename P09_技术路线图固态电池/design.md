## Research Source Hierarchy

1. **Tier 1 (Highest Confidence)**
   - Patent databases (WIPO, USPTO)
   - Academic papers (Nature Energy, Joule)
   - Official company announcements
   - Government reports and standards

2. **Tier 2 (High Confidence)**
   - Industry analyst reports (IDTechEx, Gartner)
   - Investor presentations
   - Trade publications
   - Conference papers

3. **Tier 3 (Medium Confidence)**
   - News reports from reputable sources
   - Analyst estimates
   - Company blog posts

4. **Avoid/Low Priority**
   - Rumors and speculation
   - Single-source claims
   - Unverified promotional content

## Confidence Level Assignments

- **Current technology status**: 85%+ (published research)
- **Announced timelines**: 75-85% (company statements, investor presentations)
- **Performance projections**: 60-70% (modeling, early prototypes)
- **Patent analysis**: 90%+ (published records)
- **SSB feasibility**: 85%+ (scientific validation)
- **Commercialization timing**: 50-65% (subject to delays)
- **Overall document**: 60-75%
