# Requirements: Technology Roadmap & Solid-State Battery Implications Research

## Objective
Develop comprehensive analysis of emerging battery technologies, solid-state battery requirements, and the technology roadmap for composite foil to inform CCC's R&D strategy and product positioning.

## Primary Research Questions

### 1. Lithium-Ion Battery Evolution (2025-2030)
- Key performance improvements expected in LIB technology
- High-nickel cathodes (NCM 811, 9-4-1-0) development trajectory
- LFP adoption impact on foil specifications
- Anode technology evolution (silicon-dominant, graphite evolution)
- Electrolyte evolution and solid electrolyte emergence in conventional LIB
- Mainstream adoption timeline
- Impact on current collector requirements

### 2. Solid-State Battery (SSB) Technology
- Current state of the art (2024-2025)
- Companies closest to commercialization (QuantumScape, Solid Power, Toyota, CATL, SVOLT, Samsung)
- Key technical milestones (2025-2027-2030)
- Energy density improvements (2.5x-3x vs Li-ion)
- Temperature range and safety advantages
- Cost roadmap and parity timeline with LIB

### 3. Solid-State Battery Material Requirements
- Current collector necessity analysis for SSB designs
- Foil specifications for SSB (thickness, material, surface properties)
- Bonding layer requirements (differences from LIB)
- Thermal and mechanical requirements
- Interface challenges between foil and solid electrolyte
- Differences from conventional LIB foil specifications

### 4. Competitive Technology Landscape
- Battery foil innovations being patented (2022-2025)
- Ultra-thin foil benefits and necessity (<6 μm)
- Nanocoating and durability coating technologies
- Composite foil evolution and new structures
- Technology leaders in battery foil patents and R&D

### 5. CCC's Technology Positioning
- Capabilities inherited from parent company
- Patent portfolio analysis (288 patents - domains covered)
- Current R&D focus areas
- Competitive advantages and unique capabilities
- Technology gaps vs established competitors

### 6. Technology Roadmap for Composite Foil
- Phase 1 (2025-2026): LIB performance optimization
- Phase 2 (2027-2028): SSB-compatible foil development
- Phase 3 (2029-2030): SSB production scaling
- R&D, equipment, and facility investments needed

### 7. Risk Assessment
- Technology obsolescence risk analysis
- First-mover advantage in SSB-compatible foils
- Licensing and joint venture opportunities
- Supply chain and manufacturing implications
