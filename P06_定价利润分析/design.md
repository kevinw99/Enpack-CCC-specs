## Research Strategy

### Phase 1: Current Pricing Landscape (Data Gathering)

**Objective:** Establish baseline pricing data for composite foils across regions and product types

**Data Sources:**
1. **Investor Relations Materials**
   - CCC parent company (英联股份) annual reports and presentations
   - Furukawa Electric investor presentations
   - Japanese producers' financial disclosures
   - Chinese manufacturer filings (if public)

2. **Industry Analyst Reports**
   - Grandview Research battery materials reports
   - IDTechEx copper foil market reports
   - MarketsandMarkets battery current collector reports
   - McKinsey battery cost studies
   - Seeking Alpha and other financial analyst reports

3. **Trade Publications**
   - Battery Technology articles
   - Advanced Materials & Processes
   - JOM Journal articles on battery materials
   - Industry conference proceedings

4. **Patent Literature**
   - Cost indicators from manufacturing patents
   - Production yield references
   - Material specification disclosures
   - Pricing or profitability discussions in patent claims

5. **Supply Chain Research**
   - Supplier announcements
   - Trade reports on copper and aluminum pricing
   - LME (London Metal Exchange) historical pricing
   - Regional commodity price indices

**Research Method:** Web search + document analysis

**Deliverable:** Pricing data table with:
- Composite copper foil pricing (¥/㎡, $/㎡, €/㎡)
- Composite aluminum foil pricing
- Price premiums vs commodity foil
- Quality grade pricing variations
- Regional variations (China, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Europe, US)
- Historical trends 2020-2025

**Data Sources:**
1. **Direct Cost Components**
   - Commodity prices (copper, aluminum, nickel, polymers)
   - Supplier cost information
   - Equipment cost benchmarks
   - Depreciation schedules

2. **Manufacturing Process Data**
   - Throughput specifications from equipment
   - Yield data from patents and literature
   - Scrap/defect rates by process
   - Labor requirements per stage
   - Utility consumption data

3. **Overhead Allocation**
   - CCC facility investment information
   - Estimated facility operating costs
   - Maintenance and spare parts budgets
   - Quality control costs
   - Administrative allocation

**Research Method:**
- Reverse engineering from financial statements
- Equipment supplier data sheets
- Industry benchmarking reports
- Technical literature analysis

**Deliverable:** Cost structure breakdown showing:
- Material costs as % of total (copper, aluminum, bonding layer, polymers)
- Labor costs as % of total
- Energy costs with kWh consumption data
- Overhead allocation methodology
- Gross margin calculations by product type
- Operating margin analysis
- Net margin targets

### Phase 5: Margin Drivers and Profitability Scenarios

**Objective:** Model profitability across different scenarios

**Models to Develop:**
1. **Breakeven Analysis**
   - Fixed vs variable cost breakdown
   - Breakeven volume calculations
   - Margin at different volumes (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% capacity)

2. **Sensitivity Analysis**
   - Margin sensitivity to copper/aluminum price changes
   - Margin sensitivity to volume changes
   - Margin sensitivity to yield improvements
   - Margin sensitivity to labor cost changes

3. **Scenario Modeling**
   - Market share scenarios (5%, 10%, 15% of addressable market)
   - Volume scenarios (tons per year)
   - Price scenarios (premium, competitive, cost-pressure)
   - Cost scenarios (learning curve, automation, sourcing)

4. **Return on Investment Analysis**
   - CCC capital investment: 30.89 billion yuan
   - Revenue projections: 36.71 billion yuan at capacity
   - Payback period calculations
   - IRR and NPV models

**Research Method:**
- Financial modeling based on available data
- Conservative and optimistic scenarios
- Sensitivity analysis

**Deliverable:** Profitability scenario models showing:
- Breakeven analysis
- Volume-based profitability
- Market share profitability scenarios
- Margin improvement roadmaps
- ROI analysis for capacity investments

## Data Collection Strategy

### Primary Research Approach
1. **Web Search:** Industry reports, news, investor presentations
2. **Document Analysis:** Annual reports, analyst reports, patent documents
3. **Financial Analysis:** Reverse engineering margins from public data
4. **Benchmarking:** Industry standard cost structures and margins

### Secondary Research Integration
1. **Existing Project Knowledge**
   - CCC company profile data
   - Technical specifications research
   - Manufacturing process parameters
   - Competitor profiles

2. **Cross-Reference Validation**
   - Triangulate data from multiple sources
   - Test reasonableness of calculations
   - Flag data conflicts and contradictions
   - Document confidence levels

### Quality Assurance
1. **Source Documentation:** Every data point tracked
2. **Confidence Scoring:** Each finding assessed for confidence
3. **Assumption Transparency:** All assumptions explicitly stated
4. **Peer Review:** Cross-check calculations and logic
