# Pricing Strategy and Margin Analysis for Composite Foil Manufacturing
## Requirements Document

**Project Date:** January 8, 2026
**Objective:** Develop comprehensive understanding of pricing models, margin structures, and competitive pricing dynamics for composite foil in battery current collectors

---

## Core Requirements

### R1: Current Pricing Landscape
- Document current market prices for composite copper foil (¥/㎡, $/㎡)
- Document composite aluminum foil pricing
- Quantify price premiums vs commodity foil (traditional copper/aluminum)
- Show price variations by quality grade
- Document regional price variations (China vs international)
- Track pricing trends 2020-2025

### R2: Cost Structure & Margin Analysis
- Break down material costs as % of total (copper, aluminum, bonding materials)
- Quantify labor costs as % of total production cost
- Allocate manufacturing overhead by production stage
- Calculate energy costs as percentage of total
- Estimate capital depreciation impact on per-unit margins
- Determine gross margins by product type (composite Cu, composite Al)
- Calculate operating margins (including overhead)
- Identify net margin targets for long-term profitability

### R3: Pricing Models & Strategies
- Document cost-plus pricing mechanisms
- Research value-based pricing approaches
- Analyze volume-based pricing (discount structures)
- Review long-term contract vs spot market pricing
- Examine seasonal pricing variations
- Document geography-based pricing strategies
- Analyze customer segment pricing (OEM vs distributor)
- Identify risk sharing mechanisms for commodity price pass-through

### R4: Competitive Pricing Dynamics
- Collect competitor pricing where public data available
- Analyze FURUKAWA Electric pricing and positioning
- Research Chinese manufacturer pricing (cost leaders)
- Assess price competition intensity by region
- Quantify differentiation value (quality, reliability, service)
- Estimate price elasticity of demand for composite foil

### R5: Margin Drivers & Improvement Opportunities
- Document scale effects on unit cost (learning curve data)
- Quantify yield improvement impact (scrap reduction value)
- Analyze raw material sourcing efficiency opportunities
- Identify process efficiency gains (automation, throughput)
- Calculate energy efficiency improvement potential
- Assess labor productivity improvement opportunities
- Quantify overhead absorption improvements

### R6: Customer Segment Pricing
- Define OEM pricing structure (high volume, lower margin)
- Document strategic customer pricing (long-term contracts)
- Analyze spot market pricing (short-term, higher margin)
- Research emerging market pricing strategies
- Evaluate premium product pricing (high performance)
- Document standard product pricing (cost competition)

### R7: Profitability Scenarios
- Develop breakeven analysis (fixed vs variable costs)
- Model margins at different production volumes (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)
- Analyze margin sensitivity to raw material price fluctuations
- Project profitability by market share scenarios (5%, 10%, 15%)
- Calculate return on capital investment
- Estimate payback periods for capacity investments

### R8: Pricing Evolution & Trends
- Document historical price trends 2020-2025
- Project price evolution scenarios 2025-2030
- Analyze impact of increased competition on pricing
- Assess impact of supply constraints on pricing
- Evaluate impact of new market entrants on pricing
- Examine impact of technology changes on pricing

---

## Data Quality Standards

**Confidence Target:** 65-75%
- Pricing data is inherently competitive-sensitive
- Some detailed cost structure data will be proprietary
- Target mix: 40% public data, 30% analyst estimates, 20% extrapolation, 10% assumptions

**Acceptable Data Sources:**
- Company investor presentations and annual reports
- Industry analyst reports (Grandview, IDTechEx, MarketsandMarkets, etc.)
- Trade publications (Battery Today, Industry publications)
- Patent literature (cost/process indicators)
- Supply chain research
- Public competitor financial disclosures
- Pricing databases (if available)
- Academic research on battery manufacturing economics

**Documentation Requirements:**
- Every data point must include: source, date, confidence level, methodology
- Assumptions must be explicit and justified
- Ranges should be provided when point estimates unavailable
- Regional variations clearly labeled
- Time period clearly specified for historical data

---

## Deliverable Format

Single comprehensive document with sections:

1. **Executive Summary** - Key findings and insights
2. **Current Pricing Landscape** - Detailed pricing data by product type and region
3. **Cost Structure Breakdown** - Material, labor, overhead, energy analysis
4. **Margin Analysis** - Gross, operating, and net margins by product type
5. **Competitive Pricing Positioning** - Competitor analysis and positioning
6. **Pricing Strategies by Customer Segment** - OEM, distributor, spot market, strategic customers
7. **Margin Improvement Opportunities** - Scale, yield, efficiency, sourcing improvements
8. **Profitability Scenarios** - Volume, market share, and sensitivity scenarios
9. **Pricing Trends and Evolution** - Historical and projected trends
10. **Appendices** - Detailed data tables, sources, methodology notes

---

## Success Criteria

- Document meets 65-75% confidence target
- All major data points cited with sources
- Clear documentation of assumptions and methodology
- Actionable insights for pricing strategy development
- Comprehensive competitor analysis
- Detailed cost structure breakdowns
- Multiple scenario modeling
- Clear recommendations for margin improvement
