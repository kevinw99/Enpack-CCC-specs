## Key Findings Summary

### Critical Control Points for Current Collector Foil
1. **Adhesion Quality** - Most critical; requires 100% testing or aggressive sampling (AQL 0.65-1.0)
2. **Thickness Uniformity** - Target Cpk ≥1.67 for critical characteristics
3. **Electrical Conductivity** - Must meet material specification (58% IACS aluminum, 97% copper)
4. **Surface Cleanliness** - Contamination can cause short circuits; AOI recommended
5. **Corrosion Resistance** - 96-hour salt spray test validates coating protection

### Mandatory Standards for Automotive Suppliers
- **ISO 9001:2015** - Quality management system foundation
- **IATF 16949:2016** - Automotive-specific QMS with SPC, APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA integration
- **IEC 62660** - Battery performance and safety testing
- **AEC-Q** - Component reliability and automotive qualification
- **ASTM/ISO Testing Standards** - Specific test procedures and acceptance criteria

### Industry Best Practices
- Lean manufacturing principles (eliminate waste, standardize work)
- Six Sigma methodology (DMAIC framework; target 3.4 DPMO)
- Statistical Process Control (SPC) with control charts
- Preventive maintenance schedules
- Supplier quality management and continuous improvement
