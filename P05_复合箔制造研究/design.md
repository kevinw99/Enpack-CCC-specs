# Design: Composite Foil Manufacturing Research Methodology

## Research Approach

### Phase 1: Patent Literature Review
- Google Patents search for "composite foil manufacturing"
- USPTO database search for relevant patents
- WIPO international patent database search
- Keywords: battery current collector, copper-aluminum foil, hot pressing, cold rolling, annealing

### Phase 2: Academic and Technical Literature
- IEEE Xplore for materials science and manufacturing papers
- ScienceDirect and academic databases
- Google Scholar for peer-reviewed research
- Technical conference proceedings

### Phase 3: Industry Standards and Guidelines
- International standards (ISO)
- Industry-specific standards (battery industry)
- Equipment manufacturer white papers and technical specs
- Industry association publications

### Phase 4: Competitor and Company Disclosures
- Published research from battery material suppliers
- Company technical documentation
- Product datasheets with process hints
- Trade publication articles

## Data Collection Format

For each parameter identified:
1. **Parameter Name**: Clear, specific name
2. **Value/Range**: Numerical range or specification
3. **Unit**: Measurement unit
4. **Sources**: List of 2-5 credible sources
5. **Confidence**: Percentage and rationale
6. **Notes**: Variations, material-specific details, proprietary considerations

## Documentation Standards
- All citations include: Author, Title, Publication, Date, URL/DOI
- Confidence ratings based on source credibility and consensus
- Clear notation of proprietary vs. published information
- Industry-specific terminology maintained
- Metric units preferred with common alternatives noted

## Quality Assurance Checklist
- [ ] All four process areas covered
- [ ] Each area contains 10+ parameters
- [ ] Each parameter has 2+ source citations
- [ ] Confidence levels justified and documented
- [ ] Variations by material/application noted
- [ ] Proprietary nature clearly identified
- [ ] Document organized and navigable
- [ ] References complete and accurate
