## Key Research Findings Summary

### Hot Pressing
- Temperature: 80-300°C (application-dependent)
- Pressure: 5-20 MPa (sufficient for bonding without distortion)
- Time: 5-20 minutes (thickness and material dependent)
- Key Insight: Temperature varies significantly by substrate material

### Cold Rolling
- Speed: 500-2,500 m/min (finishing mills at 2,000 m/min)
- Reduction per pass: 40-50% (optimal balance)
- Final thickness: 4.5-20 micrometers (trend toward ultra-thin)
- Tolerance: ±2-4% (depends on application)
- Key Insight: Multiple passes required to achieve final thickness

### Annealing
- Temperature: 250-500°C (300-400°C typical)
- Soak time: 30 min-8+ hours (foil thickness dependent)
- Atmosphere: N₂ or H₂/N₂ mix (prevents oxidation)
- Cooling: 1-50°C/min (controlled to prevent distortion)
- Key Insight: Atmosphere composition critical for degreasing

### Coating (Surface Treatment)
- PVD Sputtering: 0.5-25 μm, 50-200 W power, excellent adhesion
- Electroplating: 15-100 A/dm², 1-12 μm thickness
- Electroless: 50-60°C, up to 15 μm/hr deposition rate
- Surface Ra: 0.01-2.0 μm (application-dependent)
- Key Insight: Multiple coating methods available; each has specific advantages
