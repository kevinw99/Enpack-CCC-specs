# Requirements: Composite Foil Manufacturing Process Parameters Research

## Objective
Research and document industry standard manufacturing process parameters for composite copper-aluminum foil production used in battery current collectors.

## Scope
Comprehensive research covering four primary manufacturing processes:
1. Hot Pressing Process
2. Cold Rolling Process
3. Annealing Process
4. Surface Treatment/Coating Process

## Key Parameters to Research

### 1. Hot Pressing Process
- Temperature ranges (°C)
- Pressure ranges (MPa)
- Time duration (seconds)
- Line speed (m/min)
- Equipment specifications
- Material interactions

### 2. Cold Rolling Process
- Starting thickness specifications
- Target thickness ranges
- Reduction ratios per pass
- Number of passes typical range
- Line speeds (m/min)
- Hardness monitoring

### 3. Annealing Process
- Temperature profiles and peak temperatures (°C)
- Duration at peak temperature (minutes/hours)
- Atmospheric conditions (N₂, air, mixed compositions)
- Cooling rate specifications (°C/min)
- Equipment requirements

### 4. Surface Treatment/Coating Process
- Common materials used for composite layer
- Typical layer thicknesses (micrometers)
- Application methods (PVD, CVD, electroplating, sputtering, etc.)
- Quality control specifications
- Adhesion standards

## Research Sources
- IEEE and materials science journals
- Patent literature (Google Patents, WIPO, USPTO)
- Equipment manufacturer specifications
- Industry standards and technical papers
- Battery material research publications
- Published competitor disclosures

## Deliverable Requirements

### Document Structure
Create comprehensive research document with sections for each process step containing:
- Parameter name
- Typical range/value from literature
- Number of sources confirming this range
- Confidence level (70-80% target) with specific source citations
- Notes about proprietary variations

### Confidence Targets
- 70-80% confidence level for industry standards
- Document that CCC-specific parameters would be proprietary
- Clearly distinguish between published literature and proprietary processes

### Quality Standards
- Each parameter supported by at least 2-3 credible sources
- Clear citation format with publication details
- Identification of parameter variations by application or material type
- Notes on regional/standard variations if applicable

## Success Criteria
1. All four process areas comprehensively researched
2. Minimum 50+ distinct parameters documented
3. Each parameter with source citations and confidence ratings
4. Document organized and easily navigable
5. Clear distinction between industry norms and proprietary variations
6. Suitable for knowledge base integration
