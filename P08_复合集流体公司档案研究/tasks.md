## Quality Assurance Checklist
- [ ] All facts have source attribution
- [ ] All hyperlinks are functional
- [ ] No proprietary or confidential information included
- [ ] Clear distinction between confirmed/announced/projected information
- [ ] Confidence levels assigned appropriately
- [ ] No speculation beyond publicly stated information
- [ ] Document is comprehensive and well-organized
- [ ] Information is current as of January 2026

## Success Criteria
1. Document covers all 13 major sections with substantive content
2. Minimum 75% confidence level achieved (where applicable)
3. All partnership details and customer commitments documented
4. Manufacturing capacity roadmap clearly presented
5. Complete source attribution with working hyperlinks
6. Appropriate for both internal stakeholders and external partners
7. No gaps in major information categories
