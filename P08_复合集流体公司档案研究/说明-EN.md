## Strategic Vision

**Parent Company Strategy:**
- **"Second Main Business"** launch (2022-2023)
- Transition from fast-moving consumer goods packaging → new energy materials
- Dual-business model: Traditional packaging (cash generator) + Composite foils (growth engine)

**Market Opportunity:**
- Composite current collectors for lithium-ion and solid-state EV batteries
- Large and growing market as EV adoption accelerates
- Technology still in commercialization phase (2024-2026)
- Mass production potential 2026-2030+
