# CCC Company Profile Research - Requirements

## Objective
Consolidate all publicly available information about CCC (江苏英联复合集流体有限公司) and its parent company 英联股份 (Guangdong Enpack Packaging Co., Ltd.) into a comprehensive, well-sourced company profile document.

## Scope

### Information Categories to Document
1. **Company Overview**
   - Legal names (English and Chinese)
   - Founding dates
   - Parent-subsidiary relationship
   - Ownership structure and key shareholders
   - Registration details and headquarters locations
   - Company registration capital

2. **Strategic Partnerships & Customers**
   - All announced partnerships (U&S ENERGY, ULVAC, automotive companies, battery companies)
   - Partnership terms, duration, and order details
   - Customer names and supply commitments
   - Joint research initiatives

3. **Capacity and Growth Plans**
   - Current production lines operational status
   - Planned production capacity targets
   - Timeline for expansions
   - Facility locations and specifications
   - Annual production capacity projections

4. **Technology & Innovation**
   - Core product focus (composite copper foil, composite aluminum foil)
   - Technology characteristics and innovations
   - Patent portfolio
   - Solid-state battery material research
   - Joint R&D initiatives

5. **Management & Organization**
   - Executive leadership names and titles
   - Organization structure (where disclosed)
   - Key personnel backgrounds

6. **Public Announcements & News**
   - Stock exchange filings and announcements
   - Press releases and news coverage
   - Industry conference participation
   - Financial reports and investor communications
   - Awards or recognition

7. **Strategic Vision & Market Position**
   - "Second main business" strategy
   - Market positioning and competitive advantages
   - Growth targets and timelines
   - Dual-business model (easy-open lids + composite materials)

## Source Requirements
- All facts must be attributed to public sources
- Preference for official announcements and regulatory filings
- Secondary sources (news articles) acceptable with attribution
- LinkedIn profiles for executive information
- Patent databases where applicable

## Confidence Level Target
- 75-80% for overall profile (limited by confidentiality of strategic details)
- Higher confidence for announced partnerships and capacities
- Medium confidence for future projections and timelines

## Deliverable Format
- Comprehensive company profile document with:
  - Clear organization by category
  - Direct quotes from announcements where possible
  - Source citations with hyperlinks
  - Confidence levels for different claim types
  - Notes on confirmed vs. announced vs. projected information

## Research Constraints
- Public information only
- No proprietary or confidential data
- No speculative analysis beyond what's publicly stated
- Focus on facts rather than interpretation
