# CCC Company Profile Research - Design

## Document Structure

The comprehensive company profile will be organized as follows:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Quick overview of CCC and parent company
- Key facts at a glance
- Strategic positioning statement

### 2. Company Overview & History
- Legal entity information (Chinese and English names)
- Founding date and history
- Parent company information (英联股份)
- Headquarters and facility locations
- Registration details and capital structure

### 3. Business Structure & Organization
- Corporate hierarchy (parent-subsidiary relationship)
- CCC establishment date and registration details
- Ownership structure
- Registered capital
- Key subsidiaries or divisions

### 4. Core Business & Products
- Product lines:
  - Composite copper foil (MC structure)
  - Composite aluminum foil (MA structure)
- Technology characteristics
- Applications (lithium-ion batteries, solid-state batteries)
- Product specifications and differentiators

### 5. Manufacturing Capacity & Facilities
- Gaoyou facility location (高邮经济开发区)
- Current operational lines:
  - 5 composite copper foil lines
  - 5 composite aluminum foil lines
- Planned expansion:
  - Target: 100 copper foil lines
  - Target: 10 aluminum foil lines
- Annual capacity projections:
  - Copper foil: 500 million ㎡/year
  - Aluminum foil: 100 million ㎡/year
- Infrastructure (approx. 20万 sq. meters of manufacturing space)
- Timeline for capacity rollout

### 6. Strategic Partnerships & Customer Relationships
- U&S ENERGY (Korea)
  - Strategic cooperation agreement signed November 26, 2024
  - 2025 orders: 200万㎡ aluminum foil + 100万㎡ copper foil
  - 2026-2029: Increasing demand expected
  - 5-year partnership duration
  - Supplier status details

- ULVAC Partnership (Japan)
  - Equipment supplier relationship
  - Joint Research Institute establishment
  - Research focus: composite current collectors, solid-state batteries
  - Technologies: Japanese ULVAC equipment deployment

- Automotive Industry Collaborations
  - March 2025: Renowned automotive research institute partnership
  - Focus: Solid-state battery composite collector integrated materials
  - Supply commitment: Materials within 1 year

- Consumer Battery Collaborations
  - April 2025: Leading consumer battery company partnership
  - Focus: High-quality battery technology R&D
  - Details: Soft-pack lithium-ion battery applications

- Cylindrical Battery Partnership
  - August 2025: Strategic cooperation with cylindrical battery company
  - Focus: High-performance composite foil materials
  - Testing and optimization collaboration

### 7. Technology & Innovation
- Composite structure innovation (metal-polymer-metal)
- Manufacturing processes:
  - Vacuum sputtering/magnetron sputtering
  - Electroplating technology
  - One-step evaporative coating (aluminum)
- Solid-state battery material development
- Lithium metal/composite collector negative electrode integration
- Patent portfolio: 288 authorized patents (22 invention patents)
- Joint R&D focus areas

### 8. Parent Company Context (英联股份)
- Founded: 2006
- Listed on Shenzhen Stock Exchange: 2017 (Code: 002846)
- "China's #1 Easy-Open Lid Stock"
- Primary business: Metal packaging (easy-open lids for cans)
- Facilities: Shantou and Yangzhou bases
- Financial performance (2024):
  - Revenue: 20.18 billion yuan
  - Net profit: 3.97 billion yuan
  - YoY growth: 15.51% revenue, 179.15% net profit
- Strategic shift: Dual-business model (packaging + materials)

### 9. Strategic Vision & Market Position
- "Second main business" strategy launched 2022-2023
- Growth trajectory from fast-moving consumer goods to new energy materials
- Target market: Electric vehicle power lithium battery industry
- Expected revenue contribution (at full capacity): 36.71 billion yuan
- Expected tax contribution (at full capacity): 3 billion+ yuan annually
- Competitive advantages:
  - Experience with precision manufacturing
  - Lean manufacturing and digital management capabilities
  - Scale-focused expansion (100+ production lines)
  - Strategic partnerships with major OEMs

### 10. Management & Leadership
- Parent company executives (英联股份)
- CCC subsidiary leadership (where disclosed)
- Board composition and key advisors

### 11. Financial & Investment Details
- Total investment: 30.89 billion yuan
- Timeline: 2023-2025 completion target
- Funding sources and capital structure
- Parent company financial support
- Revenue and profitability projections at full capacity

### 12. Public Announcements & News Coverage
- Stock exchange filings (Shenzhen Stock Exchange - Code 002846)
- Press releases by date
- Industry conference participation
- Media coverage and news articles
- Investor relations announcements

### 13. Source Attribution & Confidence Levels
- Complete source list with hyperlinks
- Confidence rating for each major claim:
  - High confidence: Regulatory filings, official announcements
  - Medium confidence: Industry news with named sources
  - Note: Strategic details limited by confidentiality
- Data as of: January 2026 (research date)

## Information Architecture
- Chronological order where applicable (partnerships, announcements)
- Hierarchical structure (parent company → subsidiary details)
- Technical details segregated for clarity
- Financial data presented with context

## Citation Strategy
- Direct quotes from official announcements where possible
- Hyperlinked sources throughout
- Clear distinction between confirmed, announced, and projected information
- Appendix with full source list

## Audience
- Internal stakeholders seeking comprehensive company intelligence
- Potential partners evaluating collaboration opportunities
- Investors researching the company
- Strategic planners analyzing the EV battery materials market
