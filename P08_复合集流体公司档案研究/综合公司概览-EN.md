## Executive Summary

### Company Overview
**江苏英联复合集流体有限公司** (Jiangsu Yinglian Composite Current Collectors Co., Ltd.) is a subsidiary of **广东英联包装股份有限公司** (Guangdong Enpack Packaging Co., Ltd., Stock Code: 002846), a publicly listed company on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

CCC is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, production, and sales of **composite aluminum foil** and **composite copper foil** for new energy vehicle power lithium-ion batteries, with emerging focus on solid-state battery materials.

**Establishment:** February 1, 2023
**Registered Capital:** 500 million yuan
**Current Status:** Active manufacturing with 10 production lines operational
**Strategic Focus:** Composite current collectors for lithium-ion and solid-state batteries

### Parent Company Context
**英联股份** (Yinglian/Enpack) was founded in 2006 and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2017. The company is known as "China's #1 Easy-Open Lid Stock" and manufactures metal packaging products for fast-moving consumer goods. In 2022-2023, the company officially launched composite current collectors as its "second main business," representing a strategic expansion into new energy materials.

### Key Strategic Position
- Total planned investment: **30.89 billion yuan**
- Target production capacity: **500 million ㎡/year** composite copper foil + **100 million ㎡/year** composite aluminum foil
- Timeline: 2023-2025 (capacity expansion ongoing)
- Major partnerships: U&S ENERGY (Korea), ULVAC (Japan), automotive research institutes, leading battery manufacturers

## 3. Core Business & Products

### Primary Products

#### 1. Composite Copper Foil (复合铜箔 - MC Structure)
- **Structure:** PET/PP core with copper plating on both sides
- **Thickness:** Varies by specification
- **Application:** Positive electrode current collectors for lithium-ion batteries
- **Advantages:**
  - Reduced weight compared to traditional copper foil
  - Lower copper cost through composite structure
  - Enhanced thermal conductivity management
  - Scalable manufacturing process

#### 2. Composite Aluminum Foil (复合铝箔 - MA Structure)
- **Structure:** PET core with aluminum plating on both sides
- **Manufacturing Process:** One-step evaporative coating (simpler than copper foil process)
- **Application:** Negative electrode current collectors for lithium-ion batteries
- **Advantages:**
  - Optimized for negative electrode applications
  - Superior corrosion resistance
  - Weight optimization

### Technology Characteristics

**Manufacturing Process:**
- Composite copper foil: Vacuum sputtering or magnetron sputtering on PET/PI/PP film surfaces, followed by electroplating (metal layer thickness >1μm)
- Composite aluminum foil: Single-step evaporative coating process

**"Metal-Polymer-Metal" Three-Layer Composite Structure:**
The core innovation is the sandwich structure combining:
- Outer metal layer: Provides electrical conductivity
- Polymer core: Reduces weight and cost, improves thermal management
- Inner metal layer: Ensures structural integrity

### Performance Specifications
- **Thickness Range:** Optimized for battery electrode applications
- **Density:** Significantly lower than traditional foil
- **Conductivity:** Maintains required electrical properties
- **Thermal Management:** Enhanced compared to pure metal foils

### Current Product Status
- Composite copper foil: **Batch production phase** - samples being supplied to downstream battery customers
- Composite aluminum foil: Undergoing **field testing and optimization** with customers

## 6. Technology & Innovation

### Composite Current Collector Innovation

**Structural Advantages:**
1. **Weight Reduction:** Polymer core reduces overall weight compared to traditional metal foils
2. **Cost Optimization:** Composite structure uses less precious metal while maintaining conductivity
3. **Thermal Management:** Polymer interlayer improves thermal distribution
4. **Manufacturing Efficiency:** Simpler manufacturing processes for aluminum variant
5. **Scalability:** Design suitable for mass production

### Manufacturing Technology Details

**Composite Copper Foil Process:**
1. Film base: PET, PI, or PP substrate
2. Metal deposition: Vacuum sputtering or magnetron sputtering technology
3. Electroplating: Copper metal layer deposition (thickness >1μm)
4. Final product: MC-structure composite foil

**Composite Aluminum Foil Process:**
1. Film base: PET substrate
2. Metal deposition: Single-step evaporative coating (evaporation technology)
3. Final product: MA-structure composite foil

**Key Advantage:** Aluminum foil manufacturing is simpler, requiring only evaporation vs. electroplating, reducing processing steps and costs.

### Patent Portfolio

**Overall Statistics (Yinglian Group):**
- Total authorized patents: **288**
- Invention patents: **22**
- Utility model patents: Balance of 266

**R&D Focus:**
- Process development and optimization
- Academic collaboration on battery materials
- Product development cycles
- Patent protection strategy

### Solid-State Battery Material Development

**Current Focus Areas:**
1. **Lithium Metal/Composite Current Collector Negative Electrode Integration:**
   - Unified material combining lithium metal and composite collector
   - Addresses solid-state battery specific requirements
   - Potential cost and performance advantages

2. **Composite Current Collector for Solid-State Batteries:**
   - Modified material specifications for SSB electrolytes
   - Enhanced thermal and chemical stability
   - Collaboration with ULVAC on technical development

3. **Solid-State Specific Challenges:**
   - Material compatibility with solid electrolytes
   - Interface engineering between components
   - Performance under high voltage/temperature conditions

**Status:** Development phase with field testing by major customers
